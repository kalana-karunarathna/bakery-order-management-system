How did you pay?

Record the method used for this payment.

Cash

You paid cash at the bakery counter or to our delivery rider. No reference is needed. Staff confirm the payment once the cash has been counted.

Card

You paid on the bakery's card machine, in the shop or at delivery. If you have the slip, copy its reference or approval code (optional). Staff confirm the payment from the card machine report.

Bank transfer

You sent money from your bank to the bakery's account. Enter the reference shown in your banking app or on the deposit slip, often called "Reference number" or "Transaction ID". Without it, staff must find your transfer by amount and date in the bank statement, which can take longer.

Not needed for cash. Card: the reference printed on the card machine slip. Bank transfer: the reference from your bank.

Never enter a card number, expiry date or CVV. The bakery never asks for card details and does not process cards online.

Every payment is recorded as Pending. It only counts as paid after a bakery administrator has found the money (cash received, card machine report or bank statement).
